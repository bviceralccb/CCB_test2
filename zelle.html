Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get enrolled?

Follow the steps above, then enter your verification code.

How do incoming payments arrive?

Enrolled payments go into your selected account.

Already enrolled elsewhere?

Call (909) 393-8880 to move your enrollment.

What protects my information?

Existing online and mobile banking safeguards apply.

Who should I pay?

Trusted contacts only. Authorized payments have no purchase protection.

What if their bank does not offer Zelle?

Both parties now need access through a participating bank or credit union. The standalone Zelle app no longer processes payments. Read Zelle’s update.

Are overseas accounts eligible?

Both accounts must be U.S.-based.

Can payments be canceled?

Only unenrolled-recipient payments may be canceled. Report mistakes immediately.

Can I arrange future payments?

Ask us about separate scheduled-transfer services.

What does it cost?

Sending and receiving are free; pending-payment cancellation and carrier charges may apply.

What is my sending limit?

Contact us for your limits.
